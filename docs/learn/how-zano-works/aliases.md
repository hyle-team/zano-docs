# Aliases

Aliases are human-readable names registered on-chain: send to `@username` instead of a long address. One registration works across the whole ecosystem. Aliases are already used for receiving payments, authenticating with dApps, and as identities in [Zano Matrix](/docs/use/zano-matrix-guide).

Registration happens from your own wallet and costs a small on-chain fee. Names shorter than six characters are reserved (see the [alias user guide](/docs/use/aliases) for the exact rules and registration walkthrough).

An alias points at an address you chose to make public. Payments to it still use stealth addresses, so linking a name to an address does not expose your transaction history or balance.
