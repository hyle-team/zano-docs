# Getting Started

This section covers how to use Zano day-to-day.

**Get ZANO** — Buy on [Zano Trade](/docs/use/zano-trade) (the ecosystem DEX) or on the exchanges listed at [zano.org](https://zano.org). You can also earn it by [staking](/docs/stake/getting-started/proof-of-stake-mining) or [mining](/docs/mine/overview).

**Wallets** — Set up a [desktop or mobile wallet](/docs/use/wallets/overview). Then secure it: back up your [seed phrase](/docs/use/seed-phrase) and understand [password types](/docs/use/zano-passwords) in the Security & Privacy section.

**Features** — [Aliases](/docs/use/aliases) (@username addresses), [Ionic Swaps](/docs/use/ionic-swaps), [Auditable Wallets](/docs/use/auditable-wallets).

**dApps & Bridges** — Most need [Zano Companion](/docs/use/companion) (browser extension) to connect your wallet:
- [Zano Trade](/docs/use/zano-trade) — decentralized exchange (DEX)
- [Zano Matrix](/docs/use/zano-matrix-guide) — Encrypted messenger
- [Wrapped Zano](/docs/use/wrapped-zano) — ERC-20 bridge

Having problems? Check [Troubleshooting](/docs/use/troubleshooting/common-issues) or [Reporting Issues](/docs/use/reporting-issues).
